# Free Geek Loop

**Turn every corporate tech refresh into community access.**

Built for the FUSE Blueprint 2026 case competition for Free Geek Toronto (FGT).

- **Live app:** https://c1vical.github.io/free-geek-loop/
- **Video:** [YOUTUBE UNLISTED LINK]

## The problem

About 77% of FGT's revenue comes from selling refurbished tech, but its supply depends on random donations. FGT has no business partners, so it rarely gets the large, uniform batches of business laptops it can refurbish fastest.

## The solution

Free Geek Loop turns a local organization's next laptop refresh into a planned pickup:

1. **Plan:** the organization logs its next refresh date.
2. **Pledge:** devices are scored for reuse before pickup.
3. **Collect:** FGT's business pickup service brings them in.
4. **Refurbish:** data wiped, tested and repaired as one batch.
5. **Match:** laptops go to waiting nonprofit requests.
6. **Report:** the organization gets an impact report, which brings it back next refresh.

The app has three tabs: **Partner portal** (pledges and nonprofit requests), **FGT dashboard** (12-month supply forecast and pledge status) and **Impact report**.

## Run it

One HTML file, no build step. Open `index.html` or use the live link. A sample device list is in `data/stl_laptop_refresh.csv`. Demo data is illustrative.
